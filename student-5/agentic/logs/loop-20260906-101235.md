## Adapt

**Issue:** Payment errors and non-refundable payments
**Recommendation:** Implement an automated refund process that can be triggered automatically after a partial or fully refundable payment is made.
**Reason:** The current system only allows for manual refund processing, which is not scalable and prone to errors. The automatic refund process would reduce the likelihood of incorrect refunds and errors. Additionally, the automated process can be integrated with the payment system to minimize the risk of duplicate payments and improve operational efficiency.
