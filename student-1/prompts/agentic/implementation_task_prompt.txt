Customer Feedback & Reviews (Student 1 - Hangyeol Yi)

Microservices:
- student-1-database  (port 7100) owns feedback.db and exposes /db/*
- student-1-backend   (port 8100) review logic and AI-Mode, exposes /api/*
- student-1-frontend  (port 5110) HTMX screens /review and /reviews

Database Tables:
- customer_feedback: id, customer_id, customer_name, order_id,
  order_number, rating, title, comment, category, status, staff_response,
  sentiment, sentiment_score, ai_summary, ai_issues, ai_model,
  analysed_at, submitted_at, updated_at
- store_logs: id, feedback_id, entity, action, actor, actor_role, detail,
  created_at

Important domain rules:
- store_logs deliberately has NO foreign key cascade on feedback_id. Its
  purpose is to record deletions, so the log must outlive the row it
  describes. Any recommendation to add ON DELETE CASCADE is invalid.
- rating is 1 to 5 and is required. comment is required.
- The customer supplies ONLY a rating and a comment. category is derived
  from the wording by the backend.
- AI columns are written only through PUT /db/feedback/<id>/analysis, so
  a customer edit cannot forge a sentiment score. This is deliberate.
