Feature Name:
Customer Feedback & Reviews (Student 1)

Database Tables:
- customer_feedback (customer_id, order_id, rating, title, comment,
  category, status, staff_response, sentiment, sentiment_score,
  ai_summary, ai_issues, submitted_at)
- store_logs (feedback_id, action, actor, actor_role, detail, created_at)

Important domain rules:
- A customer supplies ONLY a star rating and a comment. The category is
  derived by the service, never chosen by the customer.
- A review may exist without an order_id. Not every review is linked to
  an order.
- Menu items and orders are owned by other microservices. Never suggest
  reading their tables directly.
